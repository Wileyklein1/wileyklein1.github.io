# LedgerLane

Practical tools for solo online sellers — spreadsheets, calculators, and checklists
that help Etsy, Shopify, and small e-commerce shops price right, track profit, and
run the business side without an accountant.

**Site:** https://wileyklein1.github.io/

## Free things

- **[Free Etsy profit calculator (in browser)](https://wileyklein1.github.io/calculator/)**
  — enter your price, shipping, and costs; see net profit, margin, and break-even as
  you type. Editable per-platform fee rates. No signup.
- **[Free profit calculator (spreadsheet)](https://wileyklein1.github.io/free/)** —
  the same math as an .xlsx download.

## Guides

27 plain-English guides on pricing, fees, bookkeeping, and taxes for small sellers —
[full index](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/). A few of the most-asked:

| Guide | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| [Etsy fees explained](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/etsy-fees-explained/) | Why "sold for $24" isn't "made $24" |
| [How to price handmade products](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/how-to-price-handmade-products/) | Pricing backwards from the profit you need |
| [What is COGS for handmade sellers](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/what-is-cogs-for-handmade-sellers/) | What actually counts as cost of goods |
| [Sales tax & 1099-K guide](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/online-seller-sales-tax-1099k-guide/) | What the forms mean and when they show up |
| [Quarterly estimated taxes for Etsy sellers](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/quarterly-estimated-taxes-for-etsy-sellers/) | Whether you owe them, and roughly how much |
| [Why your Etsy shop isn't getting sales](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/why-your-etsy-shop-isnt-getting-sales/) | Traffic problem or conversion problem |
| [Etsy SEO keywords guide](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/etsy-seo-keywords-guide/) | Writing titles and tags buyers actually search |
| [Etsy vs Shopify: which is more profitable](https://wileyklein1.github.io/blog/etsy-vs-shopify-which-is-more-profitable/) | Running both fee stacks against real numbers |

Fee figures change and vary by shop — verify current rates against your own account
before pricing. The tax guides are organizational, not tax advice.
