Common questions

How accurate is this profit calculator?

It uses each platform's published 2026 fee structure — Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 payment processing, and $0.20 listing fee, and the equivalent rates for Shopify, Amazon Handmade, eBay, and your own website. You can edit any rate under "Adjust fee rates" to match your exact plan. Always confirm current fees with your platform, since they change.

Does it include shipping in the fee math?

Yes. On Etsy and eBay the marketplace charges fees on the shipping you collect too, so the calculator applies fees to price plus shipping charged, then subtracts your actual shipping cost separately. That's why your net profit is often lower than a simple "price minus cost" estimate.

What margin should I aim for?

For handmade and small-batch products, aim for 35%+ net margin so promotions, returns, and ads don't wipe out your profit. Under 15% is thin and risky. This is general guidance, not financial advice.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is stored or transmitted. If you enter your email to get the free spreadsheet, that's the only thing shared, and only then.
