Questions, answered

Do these work in Google Sheets, or only Excel?

Both. Every spreadsheet is a standard .xlsx file — open it in Excel, or upload it to Google Sheets (File → Import) and it works the same way.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. You only ever type into the gold-shaded cells. Every formula, total, and chart calculates automatically — there's nothing to build.

Are the marketplace fees kept up to date?

The calculators ship pre-filled with published 2026 rates, and the fee table is fully editable in one place. Fees change often, so always verify the current numbers against your platform — the tools make that a 10-second edit.

Is the tax sheet actual tax advice?

No. It's an organizing tool that sorts your income and expenses into Schedule C categories and estimates set-asides. Always confirm figures with a qualified tax professional and current IRS guidance.

What do I actually receive?

An instant digital download — the working file(s) plus a one-page "how to use" guide. Nothing ships. You get a single-user license to use and customize the files for your own business.
